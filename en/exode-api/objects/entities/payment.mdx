---
title: "Payment and invoice"
description: "Structures of the payment, invoice, invoice item and acquiring objects based on shared zod schemas"
---

Public structures of financial objects: `payment`, `invoice`, `invoiceProduct`, `acquiring`. The full
`payment` tree arrives in the [`PaymentCompleted`](/en/exode-api/webhooks/about) webhook.

<Info>
    The [`invoice/list/raw`](/en/exode-api/school/invoice/list) method returns a compact projection of the invoice
    (`invoiceId`, `invoiceUuid`, `type`, `status`, amounts, `currency`, `user`, `products`). The full
    public schemas are below. Monetary fields are numbers (decimals from the database are cast to number).
</Info>

## `payment`

<ResponseField name="id" type="integer" required>Payment ID.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="uuid" type="string" required>Payment UUID.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="type" type="enum" required>Type: `OneTime`, `RecurrentPay`, `RecurrentInit`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status" type="enum | null">
    Status: `Created`, `WaitingPay`, `WaitingForBinding`, `Processing`, `Completed`, `BindingCompleted`, `Canceled`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="released" type="boolean" required>
    The purchase has been delivered to the user: after payment, Exode processed the invoice items — granted or extended access to
    the products.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="checkoutPaymentId" type="string | null">Payment ID in the payment system.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="checkoutUrl" type="string | null">Payment URL.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="paidAt" type="string | null">Payment date (ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="expireAt" type="string | null">Expiration date (ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="isCompleted" type="boolean | null">The payment is completed.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="isCanceled" type="boolean | null">The payment is canceled.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="meta" type="object | null">Internal metadata.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="webhookLogs" type="object[] | null">Payment system webhook logs.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="chargeLogs" type="object[] | null">Charge logs.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="statusHistoryLogs" type="object[] | null">Status history: `{ timestamp, status }`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="acquiring" type="object | null">Acquiring (see below).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="invoice" type="object | null">Invoice (see below).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string" required>Creation date (ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string" required>Update date (ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="archivedAt" type="string | null">Archiving date or `null`.</ResponseField>

## `invoice`

<ResponseField name="id" type="integer" required>Invoice ID.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="uuid" type="string" required>Invoice UUID.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="humanId" type="integer | null">Human-readable invoice number.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="type" type="enum" required>Type: `Regular`, `InstallmentPay`, `InstallmentInit`, `SubscriptionPay`, `SubscriptionInit`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status" type="enum | null">Status: `Active`, `Canceled`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="totalAmount" type="number" required>Total amount.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="discountAmount" type="number" required>Discount amount.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="currency" type="enum" required>Currency: `Free`, `Exes`, `Rub`, `Uzs`, `Kzt`, `Usd`, `Eur`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="expireAt" type="string | null">Expiration date (ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="isActive" type="boolean | null">The invoice is active.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="meta" type="object | null">Internal invoice metadata.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="user" type="object | null">
    The buyer — a [user with profile](./user) object; may additionally contain `school`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="products" type="object[] | null">Invoice items (see `invoiceProduct`).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string" required>Creation date (ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string" required>Update date (ISO 8601).</ResponseField>

## `invoiceProduct`

<ResponseField name="id" type="integer" required>Item ID.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="originalPrice" type="number" required>Original price.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="totalPrice" type="number" required>Total price.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="discountAmount" type="number" required>Discount amount for the item.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="price" type="object | null">Product price — a [`productPrice`](./product) object.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="discount" type="object | null">Discount — a [`discount`](./product) object.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="product" type="object | null">Product — a [`product`](./product) object; may additionally contain `course`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string" required>Creation date (ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string" required>Update date (ISO 8601).</ResponseField>

## `acquiring`

<ResponseField name="id" type="integer" required>Acquiring ID.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="uuid" type="string" required>Acquiring UUID.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="active" type="boolean | null">Active.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="name" type="string | null">Name.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="description" type="string | null">Description.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="hasProviderCommission" type="boolean | null">Whether the provider charges a commission.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="provider" type="object | null">Provider: `{ id, type, name, active }`.</ResponseField>

<Note>
    Provider secrets (apiKey, configuration) and the payment method/card number are not returned in the public schema.
</Note>
