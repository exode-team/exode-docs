## Setup

Paste the standard Meta Pixel snippet with your Pixel ID into the school's [custom code](/en/customization/js-config) (**For developers → Custom code**, HTML tab).
The platform detects `window.fbq` and starts sending events automatically.

## Events sent

[Conversion events](/en/analytics/events) are mapped to
[Meta standard events](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/402791146561655)
(which support automatic ad optimization) and two custom events:

### Sign-up

<ParamField body="CompleteRegistration" type="track">
    Registration of a new account. The `registration_method` parameter is `email` or `phone`.
</ParamField>

### Lead (onboarding form)

<ParamField body="Lead" type="track">
    The onboarding form is filled in.
</ParamField>

### Course view

<ParamField body="ViewContent" type="track">
    Course page view. Parameters: `content_type: 'product'`, `content_ids`.
</ParamField>

### Demo lesson opened

<ParamField body="ViewDemoLesson" type="trackCustom">
    A demo lesson is opened. Parameters: `content_ids`, `lesson_id`. A custom event:
    you can set up a custom conversion for it in Events Manager.
</ParamField>

### Free course enrollment

<ParamField body="StartTrial" type="track">
    Enrollment in a free course. Parameter: `content_ids`.
</ParamField>

### Checkout initiated

<ParamField body="InitiateCheckout" type="track">
    Proceeding to checkout. Parameters: `num_items`, `content_ids`.
</ParamField>

### Purchase

<ParamField body="Purchase" type="track">
    Successful payment. Parameters: `value`, `currency` (ISO 4217), `content_type`, `content_ids`.
    The invoice uuid is passed as `eventID`: Meta itself merges duplicates with the same `eventID`
    within 48 hours. The event is not sent for payments in internal currencies (points), because
    Meta requires a real currency.
</ParamField>

### Course completion

<ParamField body="CompleteCourse" type="trackCustom">
    Course completion. Parameter: `content_ids`. A custom event.
</ParamField>
