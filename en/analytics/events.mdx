## Accuracy and transparency

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="No duplicates" icon="shield-check">
    A purchase is sent once per invoice: an on-device guard plus `eventID` / `transaction_id`
    for deduplication on the Meta and GA4 side.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Real money only" icon="coins">
    Amounts and currencies come from the invoice (ISO 4217); internal points do not distort revenue and ROAS.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fully transparent" icon="eye">
    Events are regular `document` events: you can see them in the console and intercept them with your own code.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Event list

Each event is dispatched as a `CustomEvent` on `document` (data is in `event.detail`), so
you can intercept it with your own code for [any other platform](/en/analytics/custom).

### Sign-up

<ParamField body="analytics:signup-completed" type="event">
    Registration of a new account via email or phone. The event is not sent when signing in via social networks (VK, Telegram)
    or OTP, because these methods do not yet distinguish a new user from a returning sign-in.

    <Expandable title="event.detail fields">
        <ParamField body="method" type="enum">Registration method: `email`, `phone`.</ParamField>
    </Expandable>
</ParamField>

### Lead (onboarding form)

<ParamField body="profile:personal-info-filled-success" type="event">
    The user filled in the onboarding form (name and personal data). No `event.detail`.
</ParamField>

### Course view

<ParamField body="analytics:course-viewed" type="event">
    Course page view.

    <Expandable title="event.detail fields">
        <ParamField body="courseId" type="number">Course ID.</ParamField>
    </Expandable>
</ParamField>

### Demo lesson opened

<ParamField body="analytics:demo-lesson-opened" type="event">
    A demo lesson is opened from the course page.

    <Expandable title="event.detail fields">
        <ParamField body="courseId" type="number">Course ID.</ParamField>
        <ParamField body="lessonId" type="number">Demo lesson ID.</ParamField>
    </Expandable>
</ParamField>

### Free course enrollment

<ParamField body="analytics:free-course-enrolled" type="event">
    Enrollment in a free course.

    <Expandable title="event.detail fields">
        <ParamField body="productId" type="number">Course product ID.</ParamField>
    </Expandable>
</ParamField>

### Checkout initiated

<ParamField body="analytics:checkout-initiated" type="event">
    The user proceeds to checkout (a pricing plan is selected).

    <Expandable title="event.detail fields">
        <ParamField body="productId" type="number">Product ID.</ParamField>
        <ParamField body="priceId" type="number">Selected pricing plan ID.</ParamField>
    </Expandable>
</ParamField>

### Purchase

<ParamField body="analytics:purchase-completed" type="event">
    Successful invoice payment. Sent **once per invoice**: resending on the same device
    is blocked for 90 days by the invoice uuid, and if the page is opened late (another device,
    a link from an email, more than 5 minutes after payment), the event is not sent at all.

    <Expandable title="event.detail fields">
        <ParamField body="value" type="number">Invoice amount.</ParamField>
        <ParamField body="currency" type="enum">Invoice currency: `Rub`, `Uzs`, `Kzt`, `Usd`, `Eur`, `Exes`, `Free`.</ParamField>
        <ParamField body="invoiceUuid" type="string">Invoice uuid: a unique identifier for deduplication.</ParamField>
        <ParamField body="productIds" type="number[]">IDs of the paid products.</ParamField>
    </Expandable>
</ParamField>

### Course completion

<ParamField body="analytics:course-completed" type="event">
    The user completed a course.

    <Expandable title="event.detail fields">
        <ParamField body="productId" type="number">Course product ID.</ParamField>
    </Expandable>
</ParamField>
